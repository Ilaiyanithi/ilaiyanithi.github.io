About Me

I am a Digital Marketing Specialist and Team Lead with 1.9+ years of experience in B2B and SaaS marketing across paid media, SEO, social media, and marketing automation. Currently at Dikshi Technologies, I lead a three-member team managing end-to-end campaign execution, SEO, paid ads, and reporting.

Passionate about building marketing systems, I developed in-house tools including a Technical SEO Audit Tool for automated crawling and indexability checks, and a Live Lead Tracking Mini CRM integrated with Google Sheets for real-time campaign attribution.
